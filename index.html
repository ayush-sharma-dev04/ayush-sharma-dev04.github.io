A little about me

I’m Ayush — a Computer Science student at Girijananda Chowdhury University, Guwahati, originally from Dimapur, Nagaland.

I’m not a working engineer; I’m someone who learns by building. Most of my curiosity lands in technology, AI and computer vision, and the best way I’ve found to understand any of it is to make a small, working version and see where it breaks.

That usually means pose-tracking experiments, a food-recognition model trained on too few images, or a RAG chain I keep rewriting. I like the part where an idea stops being a note and starts running.
